1. Take the photos

  1. Put the t-shirt on a flat surface with a colour that contrasts with the t-shirt.
  2. Make the t-shirt flat with your hands. Remove all folds that are not part of the garment.
  3. Put the marker on the surface, near the t-shirt. Do not put the marker on the t-shirt.
  4. Hold the camera directly above the t-shirt.
  5. Make sure that the full t-shirt and the full marker are in the frame.
  6. Take the front photo.
  7. Turn the t-shirt over. Do steps 2 to 5 again. Take the back photo.
  8. Sleeve photo: fold one sleeve flat along its underarm line. Put the fold on the left and the cap at the top. Do steps 3 to 5 again. Take the sleeve photo.

The marker

  1. Open the marker PDF.
  2. Set the print scale to 100% ("Actual size").
  3. Print the marker on matte paper.
  4. Measure the black square with a steel ruler. The side must be 100 mm, plus or minus 0.5 mm.
  5. Attach the marker to stiff card. Make sure that the card is flat.
Sleeve photo rule. The same rule holds for the front and back photos: the whole t-shirt and the whole marker are in the frame.

2. Make a pattern

No photos yet? Use a synthetic tee. It has known measurements, so the page shows the error.

REAL mode spends money on every run. Each run is checked against the budget limit.

3. Check the proof photo

Cut the paper panel on the stitch line and put it on the t-shirt with its fold on the centre. Take one photo from above, with the marker in frame. The check uses the pattern of the last run.

The sample proof is a paper panel cut 1% too big, for the sample tee.
