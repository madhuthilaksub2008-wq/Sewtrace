Step 01 · Capture

Take the photos

Three photos from above: front, back and one folded sleeve. The printed marker gives the scale, so every length on the pattern comes out in real millimetres.

How to take them

3 photos
  1. Put the t-shirt on a flat surface with a colour that contrasts with the t-shirt.
  2. Make the t-shirt flat with your hands. Remove all folds that are not part of the garment.
  3. Put the marker on the surface, near the t-shirt. Do not put the marker on the t-shirt.
  4. Hold the camera directly above the t-shirt.
  5. Make sure that the full t-shirt and the full marker are in the frame.
  6. Take the front photo.
  7. Turn the t-shirt over. Do steps 2 to 5 again. Take the back photo.
  8. Sleeve photo: fold one sleeve flat along its underarm line. Put the fold on the left and the cap at the top. Do steps 3 to 5 again. Take the sleeve photo.
Tips for good photos
  • Tape the marker card flat to the surface.
  • Put the marker close to the t-shirt, near the middle of one side. Do not put the marker on the t-shirt.
  • Hold the camera directly above the t-shirt.
  • Put the t-shirt in the middle of the frame.
  • Use the main lens. Do not use the wide-angle lens.
  • Put each sleeve at an angle down from the shoulder. Keep a gap between the sleeve and the side of the t-shirt.
  • For the sleeve photo, keep the sleeve sewn on. Fold one sleeve flat along its underarm line, with the fold on the left and the cap at the top. Turn the t-shirt so the folded sleeve lies below the body. Keep the whole t-shirt and the marker in the photo.
The marker

The marker

  1. Open the marker PDF.
  2. Set the print scale to 100% ("Actual size").
  3. Print the marker on matte paper.
  4. Measure the black square with a steel ruler. The side must be 100 mm, plus or minus 0.5 mm.
  5. Attach the marker to stiff card. Make sure that the card is flat.

Step 05 · Check the fit

Does the paper match the t-shirt?

An optional last check. Lay the printed pattern on the t-shirt and take one photo; the page measures the gap between the paper edge and the t-shirt edge all the way round.
