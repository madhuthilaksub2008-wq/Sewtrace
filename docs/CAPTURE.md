# Capture tips

Use these tips with the photo steps in SPEC 9.2. The web page shows the same tips.

1. Tape the marker card flat to the surface.
2. Put the marker close to the t-shirt, near the middle of one side. Do not put the marker on the t-shirt.
3. Hold the camera directly above the t-shirt.
4. Put the t-shirt in the middle of the frame.
5. Use the main lens. Do not use the wide-angle lens.
6. Put each sleeve at an angle down from the shoulder. Keep a gap between the sleeve and the side of the t-shirt.

## Why

- A curled card, a marker far from the t-shirt, a camera that is not above the t-shirt and a
  wide-angle lens all change the scale at the t-shirt. The marker check cannot see this error.
- The program finds the shoulder point where the sleeve top bends away from the shoulder seam.
  If the sleeve top and the shoulder seam are nearly in line, the program cannot place the
  point. It stops and names the photo. Put the sleeve at a wider angle from the shoulder and take that photo again.
